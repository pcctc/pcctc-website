The PCCTC plans on hiring three summer interns next year to support [Data Management](https://msk.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/MSKCC_Careers_Primary/job/New-York---Teleworker/Data-Management-Summer-Intern_93445), [Data Science](https://msk.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/MSKCC_Careers_Primary/job/New-York---Teleworker/Data-Science-Summer-Intern_93446), and [Clinical Operations](https://msk.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/MSKCC_Careers_Primary/job/New-York---Teleworker/Clinical-Operations-Summer-Intern_93447). The 10-week internships are full-time, paid, 100% remote, and working hours are 9-5 EST. The dates are flexible; approximate dates are June – August 2026.

-

The **Data Management Summer Intern** will work directly with the Data Management team, gaining hands-on experience with the data review aspects of clinical research including query management, data cleaning and reconciliation. The intern will also have the opportunity meet and learn from staff in other PCCTC divisions, such as Business Operations, Clinical Operations, Data Sciences, Correlative Sciences and Monitoring.

-

The **Data Science Summer Intern** will work directly with the PCCTC’s Data Science team, gaining hands-on experience with the data oversight and reporting aspects of clinical research within the framework of R programming and version control with git & GitHub. The intern will also have the opportunity meet and learn from staff in other PCCTC divisions, such as Business Operations, Data Management, Clinical Operations, Correlative Sciences and Monitoring.

-

The **Clinical Operations Summer Intern** will work directly with the Clinical Operations team, gaining hands-on experience with the aspects of both early and late phase clinical research studies, including protocol development, site selection and oversight, patient and regulatory management. The intern will also have the opportunity meet and learn from staff in other PCCTC divisions, such as Business Operations, Data Management, Data Sciences, Correlative Sciences and Regulatory/Monitoring.

For further information or to apply please follow links above.
