The Prostate Cancer Clinical Trials Consortium (PCCTC) is pleased to announce its Summer 2025 internships. Opportunities are available in [Data Management](https://externaltalent-mskcc.icims.com/jobs/83000/pcctc-data-management-internship%2c-remote/job), [Data Science](https://externaltalent-mskcc.icims.com/jobs/83004/pcctc-data-science-internship%2c-remote/job), and [Clinical Operations](https://externaltalent-mskcc.icims.com/jobs/83005/pcctc-clinical-operations-internship%2c-remote/job). The 10-week internship is full-time, paid, 100% remote, and working hours are 9-5 EST. The dates are flexible; approximate dates are June – August 2025. Interested candidates pursuing a career in clinical research or other health professions are encouraged to click the linked internship descriptions to learn more.
